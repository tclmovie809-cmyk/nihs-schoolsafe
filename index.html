🚨 NEED IMMEDIATE HELP?

If you are in immediate danger

Move to a safe place and seek help from a trusted adult, teacher, school staff, parent, guardian, or appropriate emergency services.

Remember

NIHS SCHOOLSAFE is designed to help students report safety concerns. It should not replace emergency assistance when someone is in immediate danger.

🔒 PRIVACY & SAFETY

Your Privacy Matters

NIHS SCHOOLSAFE is designed to provide students with a safer way to communicate concerns.

Confidential Reporting

Information submitted through the system should only be accessible to authorized school personnel responsible for handling student safety concerns.

📚 KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Every Student Deserves Safety

Students have the right to learn in an environment where they are treated with respect and dignity.

You Can Speak Up

If you experience or witness bullying, harassment, threats, discrimination, or another safety concern, you should be able to seek help from trusted adults and school personnel.
