🚨 Need Immediate Help?

If you are in immediate danger, move to a safe place and tell a trusted teacher, school staff member, parent, guardian, or other trusted adult immediately.


NIHS SCHOOLSAFE is for reporting and documenting student safety concerns. It should not replace emergency assistance.


🔒 Privacy & Safety

Your Privacy

You may choose to submit a report anonymously. Only information necessary for handling the concern should be provided.

Safety

Do not include passwords, account credentials, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information in your report.


📚 Know Your Rights

Right to Safety

Every student deserves a safe learning environment.

Right to Be Heard

Students should be able to report concerns and have their concerns taken seriously.

Right to Privacy

Personal information should be handled responsibly and only used when necessary.


ℹ️ NIHS SCHOOLSAFE

NIHS SCHOOLSAFE is a Student Safety & Reporting System designed for New Israel High School.


Its purpose is to provide students with a simple way to report safety concerns and help the school respond appropriately.
